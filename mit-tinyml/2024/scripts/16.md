But this is not enough, because just using this token, the generated image quality is not quite good. We need to also add this residual diffusion model to predict the difference between the original image and the sum of predicted tokens at different resolutions (like 1x1, 2x2, 3x3, 4x4). We add them together. The task of the small diffusion model is the difference between the sum of discrete tokens and the final output. This becomes quite easy, and only 30 or 40 million parameters is enough to complete this task—to let the residual diffusion learn these residual tokens. Finally, we add the residual tokens with the original discrete tokens to get the final output, and that's how we achieve such high-fidelity visual generation and also achieve high throughput.
