For Google, they have the AlphaQubit paper using transformer-based models. Before that, we also had a paper, Transformer QC, proposing a transformer-based decoder for the surface code. We have influence on the algorithm side, especially for the decoder part. For hardware, we try to find impactful ways to collaborate, for example, with Dr. Will Oliver's group here, working on placement of qubits with automated processes, and in the future, possibly a tape-out of qubits with fully automated design using machine learning methods.
